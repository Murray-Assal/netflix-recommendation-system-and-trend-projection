What Netflix added in 2020-21 looks different from what it added up to 2017: more comedy, drama and romance, far fewer documentaries and stand-up specials, and new voices from Nigeria, Egypt and Southeast Asia.

Compared by the year a title was added: up to 2017 against 2020-2021 (2021 runs to 25 Sep). Share means the percentage of titles in that period that carry the genre, or come from that country, so shares are not affected by the catalogue growing. The year filter does not apply on this view.

Titles by release year rose to a peak of 744 in 2017 and have fallen since. The best short-term forecast is flat to slightly down, about 430 to 500 a year, and nothing beyond two years is reliable.

Models were trained on complete years 2000-2020 and scored on a rolling backtest (train through year t, predict the next one to three years, origins 2012-2019). The 2021 point is the 219 titles recorded to 25 Sep, so the full year cannot be seen. This series counts titles in this catalogue snapshot by release year; it is not Netflix's production volume.

Two models were built from the metadata. A classifier predicts a title's audience level (Adult, Teen or Family) with 67.6% accuracy against 48.3% for guessing the biggest class. A recommender finds same-genre titles even when genre is hidden from it.

Pick a title to see its ten closest matches. Similarity is cosine similarity on genres, director, country, rating, type and release decade, with genres weighted most.

    Six recommendations, each tied to the evidence in this data. All are supply-side: the file holds no viewing, cost or revenue figures, so each one names the test that would confirm it.

    Data: 5,915 Netflix titles, added through 25 Sep 2021. Audience level is derived from the rating (Adult: TV-MA, R, NC-17; Teen: TV-14, PG-13; Family: TV-PG, PG, TV-G, G, TV-Y, TV-Y7). 78 unrated titles are left out of audience views. Charts by year start in 2015; 45 titles were added earlier. Models and forecasts come from the earlier analysis tasks and are re-used here unchanged.
